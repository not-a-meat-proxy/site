# Your first topic

A topic holds one piece of work: its goal, the canvas its session writes, the questions it asks you and the threads you hold with it.

## Open the page

Open the address `namp start` printed, `http://localhost:4321/__session/`. It opens on Home, whose Digest lists what waits for you across your topics, the most urgent first: click a row to read it whole and act on it there. The rail at the left goes to Topics, Projects, Machines, Agents and Settings, and **Chat**, at the top right of Home, is where you talk to the Main session.

## Start a topic

Press **New topic**, at the top right of Home, and fill in:

- **Title**, the topic's name.
- **Project** or **Folder**, where the session works. A project is a git clone you register once in Projects: each topic on it works in a worktree of its own, on a new branch. A folder is any absolute path.
- **Runs as**, **Skills** and **Siblings**: the agent the session runs as, its skills and the sessions it may start for parts of the work. The defaults are a good start.
- **Goal**, in one line: what the work is meant to reach.
- **Brief**: how the session should work towards it, and what it should produce.

Then press **Start the session**. namp opens the session in a new herdr tab and its topic in the page. Its first move is to propose the goal: the strip under the topic's top bar shows it as proposed, with **Accept**. The session starts the work once you accept it.

## Answer a question

When a session needs a decision, it asks you a question: a title, its context, numbered choices with the one it recommends marked, and **Your own answer** last. A question shows where the session links it on the canvas, on Home, and in its thread.

Pick a choice with a click or its number, or type your own answer, then send it with the arrow or Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on a Mac. The session gets it at once and goes on. To change your mind, pick another choice.

## The Feed

A topic's conversation opens in the drawer at the right of its page, from its top bar. **Feed** lists the threads holding a message for you that you have not acknowledged: questions, replies to you, anything a session wrote to you. **All** lists every thread, the sessions' talk among themselves included.

When you are done with a thread, acknowledge it, with your chip in the thread's bar or `a`: it leaves the Feed until a new message comes. Opening a thread does not acknowledge it, so nothing slips by. On Home, `j` and `k` move between the rows, `a` acknowledges one and `o` opens its topic.

Press `?` anywhere on the page for every action and its key.
