# Your first topic

A topic holds one piece of work: its goal, the plan its session follows towards it, the canvas the session writes, the questions it asks you and the threads you hold with it.

## Open the page

Open the address `namp start` printed, `http://localhost:4321/__session/`. It opens on Home, a briefing across your topics since you left:

- **Needs you**: only what waits for you to act, such as a question to answer, a goal to accept or a session that is blocked, each answerable right there. **Open in topic** takes you to it.
- **While you were away**: what came of each topic, what was done, decided, waits on you or is blocked, each marked at its left by who started it: you, the session, or a task the session handed out.
- **Running now** and **Next**: what the sessions work on, and what their plans hold next.

The rail at the left goes to Topics, Projects, Machines, Agents and Settings, and counts what needs you on Home. **Chat**, at the top right of Home, is where you talk to the Main session.

## Start a topic

Press **New topic**, at the top right of Home, and fill in:

- **Title**, the topic's name.
- **Project** or **Folder**, where the session works. A project is a git clone you register once in Projects: each topic on it works in a worktree of its own, on a new branch. A folder is any absolute path.
- **Runs as**, **Skills** and **Siblings**: the agent the session runs as, its skills and the sessions it may start for parts of the work. The defaults are a good start.
- **Goal**, in one line: what the work is meant to reach.
- **Brief**: how the session should work towards it, and what it should produce.

Then press **Start the session**. namp opens the session in a new herdr tab and its topic in the page. Its first move is to propose the goal: the strip under the topic's top bar shows it as proposed, with **Accept**. The session starts the work once you accept it.

## Read a topic

A topic opens on its **Overview**, its canvas a view beside it in the top bar. From the top:

- its goal, each criterion ticked or still open;
- **Needs you**, what waits for you in this topic;
- its **Plan**, the work running, then planned, then what is done and dropped, folded;
- its work today as a timeline, a lane for you, the session and each session it started;
- **Since you were here**, everything else that happened, the newest first: work started and done, decisions the session took, messages and what they led to, changes to the canvas.

Nothing there asks you to mark it read: once it has been in view, it counts as seen, and the next time you come back the record starts after it. Click a piece of work to see where it came from and what it did, and a reference to a thread, a part of the canvas or a decision to unfold it in place. **Fold all** folds them again.

When the session decides something without asking you, it says so in the record, with what it chose and why. If you would have chosen otherwise, press **Overrule**: the decision comes back to you as a question.

## Answer a question

When a session needs a decision, it asks you a question: a title, its context, numbered choices with the one it recommends marked, and **Your own answer** last. A question shows where the session links it on the canvas, under Needs you, and in its thread.

Pick a choice with a click, or its number once the question has the focus, or type your own answer, then send it with the arrow or Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on a Mac. The session gets it at once and goes on. To change your mind, pick another choice.

## The conversation

A topic's conversation opens in the drawer at the right of its page, from **Conversation** in its top bar. **Needs you** lists the threads waiting for you to act. **All** lists every thread, the sessions' talk among themselves included. `j` and `k` move between the threads, and `Esc` hides the drawer.

## Close a topic

When the work is done, **Close the topic**, from the **More** menu, ⋯ at the right of its top bar, archives it. It leaves Home and the topic list for **Archived**, linked under your topics in the sidebar, where it stays readable as it was: its canvas, its threads, its plan and its record. **Reopen** brings it back to work in, and **Delete** removes it for good.
